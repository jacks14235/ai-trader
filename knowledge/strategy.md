# Current Strategy

This paper portfolio searches broadly, then concentrates attention on a bounded candidate slate.
It favors evidence-backed dislocations, event-driven opportunities, misunderstood second-order
beneficiaries, and beaten-up businesses where the facts support a credible change in trajectory.

Daily decisions should compare new ideas with current holdings and cash, explain what the market
may be missing, identify the strongest counterargument, and define observable invalidation
conditions. No action is valid when the evidence does not support a clear asymmetric opportunity.

This document is human-owned for now. A future weekly strategist may propose versioned edits, but
no agent may silently rewrite it and no strategy edit may weaken deterministic risk controls.
